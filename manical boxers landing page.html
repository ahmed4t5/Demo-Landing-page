Clarification

Everything you
need to know.

What if I am out of shape right now?

Perfect. That is why you start. Our foundational classes scale intensity to your current work capacity so you build up safely.

Do I need to buy gear before my trial class?

No. We supply loaner gloves and hand wraps for your first session. Just bring athletic wear and a water bottle.

Will I get hit or have to spar?

Not unless you choose to. Sparring is strictly optional and reserved for advanced technical tracks. Fundamentals focus on pad work, bag work, and partner drills.

How many times a week should a beginner train?

2 to 3 sessions per week is the sweet spot for rapid skill retention and optimal recovery.
